# Assumptions to confirm with Marie

Places where the screens did not say, or where the build had to choose. Each line is a guess. Marie: confirm or correct, and we change it.

## Wording and labels
- Labels stay in French where Marie's screens show English ("UI Themes", "Buy / Equip" become "Thèmes", "Acheter / Équiper"). Proper names are never translated.
- "Top Theme of the Week" is shown as "À la une" and picks the best-rated item, not a weekly vote.
- The quest buttons read "Lancer la quête", "Reprendre la quête", "Revoir la quête" everywhere, including the Cité home page.
- Greeting on La Cité: "Salam, et bienvenue chez vous".

## La Cité (welcome page)
- "Charte de La Cité" was a link to the guide; the small guide links were removed from the screens and the guide stays in the account menu.
- The three communities on the home page are the first public ones, not a curated choice.
- The "first steps" checklist uses our own definitions of each step.

## Quêtes
- The quest of the day changes at midnight UTC (same as Dakar in winter).
- The daily quest shows XP and Kopeks; the daily bonus amounts are placeholders (0) until the real values are decided.
- Streak card: a day counts when at least one quest is solved that day; the chest reward text is a placeholder.
- A missed day cannot be made up: rewards belong to the day.
- Quests take 10 minutes at most.

## Le Souk
- Nine items as on Marie's screen: three themes, three frames, three badges. Prices in the demo are very low on purpose, for testing.
- A theme sets the accent, background, glow, panels and lines; a creator's theme sets only the accent.
- The default look is the neon theme; the gold theme ("Sahel Doré") is the first alternative.
- Buying a theme lets the member switch to it and back at any time.

## Communautés
- Six topics: web, IA et données, gaming, design, mobile, open source. Up to four tags per community. The activity line is at most 80 characters.
- Project rooms (communities attached to a project) are not listed under "Mes communautés".

## Parcours (learning paths)
- One path per clan to start; anyone can follow any clan's path.
- A step finishes by solving its quest, or by marking the lesson as read when it has no quest.
- No Figma screen exists for paths: the layout is ours.

## Navigation
- Top bar: La Cité, Projets, Quêtes, Le Souk, Jobs. Side rail: Communautés, joined communities and rooms, Blog, Questions, Parcours.
- The logo leads to La Cité.
- Projets has a Communauté / Espace personnel switch.

## Profiles
- The Blog tab shows on every profile ("Mes blogs" on your own, "Blog" on others).
