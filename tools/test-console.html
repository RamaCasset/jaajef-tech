jaajef.tech test console

A private page for testing the real database with three accounts. It is not part of the product. Nothing is sent anywhere except to your own Supabase project.

Do not paste the secret or service key here. Only the project address and the public (anon / publishable) key.

1. Connect

Supabase may have given your functions random names such as bright-action. Type the real names here (see Edge Functions in Supabase). The name is the last part of the function's address.

In Supabase: Project Settings, then API. For quick testing, turn off "Confirm email" under Authentication, then Sign In / Providers, then Email. Turn it back on before launch.

2. The three accounts

Use three different email addresses (with Gmail, you can use yourname+ama@gmail.com, yourname+bara@gmail.com and yourname+coumba@gmail.com). Passwords: at least 8 characters. Press Sign up once, then Sign in whenever you reload this page.
